The technical foundation involves using mmap to allocate page-aligned memory, integrating with Metal's GPU API to wrap pointers without copies, and leveraging Wasmtime's MemoryCreator trait to provide custom memory backing. The article highlights applications in portable key-value caches, allowing serialization and restoration of transformer contexts with 5.45× speedup at 24 tokens, and explores implications for actor mobility and snapshot-based inference across machines. While promising for efficient, sandboxed AI deployment, challenges remain in scaling to larger models and ensuring compatibility, reflecting broader trends toward hardware-accelerated, secure runtime environments for generative AI.
